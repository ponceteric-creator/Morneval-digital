# Intrigue Deck Composition Principles

**Status:** Locked design principle for future physical deck composition and playtest calibration.

## Powerful-card availability

Each Institution deck must be composed so that a Family with normal access to that deck can reasonably expect to have access to at least one **powerful Intrigue card** approximately once every **two Generations**.

For current deck-composition work, a **powerful card** is provisionally defined as a card whose meaningful play mode costs **4 Influence or more**. Variable-cost cards count when their 4+ Influence mode represents a genuine high-impact use rather than a merely inflated version of a minor effect.

This is an **availability target**, not a guarantee that the Family can always afford or legally play the card when drawn.

Because Agent seniority increases the number of cards seen before keeping one, deck composition should not require 50% of all physical cards to be powerful. As an initial calibration target, approximately **25–30% of each Institution deck** should be powerful cards.

With one Seniority-2 Agent (draw 2, keep 1), a powerful-card density of:

- **25%** gives about a **44%** chance of seeing at least one powerful card in a Generation and about **68%** across two Generations;
- **30%** gives about a **51%** chance in one Generation and about **76%** across two Generations.

Multiple Agents and higher seniority increase this availability substantially, so the density should not be pushed much above one third without playtest evidence.

## Recurring access

Permanent unique cards leave normal circulation once played. Therefore a deck should not rely only on Permanent cards to meet the powerful-card frequency target. Each Institution should contain at least one **non-Permanent / recyclable** high-impact card capable of satisfying the 4+ Influence power-card criterion.

## Current Merchant clarifications

- **Licence Revocation / Audit of Privileges** is Active: Action, Non-Permanent, cost 2 Influence. Each opposing Family pays 1 Influence per Merchant Guild Agent; for each unpaid point it removes one of its own Merchant Guild Agents. Agent seniority does not modify the payment.
- **Merchant Lobbying** is Parked pending redesign of the City Inclination system.
