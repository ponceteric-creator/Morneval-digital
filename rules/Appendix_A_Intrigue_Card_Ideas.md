# Appendix A — Intrigue Card Ideas

**Status:** Design appendix — proposed cards and effect families, not final balanced rules.  
**Related rules:** MOR-2.25 Intrigue Cards; MOR-4.17 Intrigue Themes and Generic Contributions.  

This appendix records Intrigue-card concepts proposed during design discussion so that they can be tested later without treating every numerical value, timing window or cost as locked. The working target is approximately **5–6 distinct effects per Institution**, with controlled overlap between Institutions: similar mechanical families may appear in more than one deck, but should normally apply to different targets or operate through different thematic means.

## A.1 — Shared design families

Several card families may exist in all four Institutions with institution-specific names and/or execution.

### Institutional Development
A card that facilitates development of its own Institution, normally by reducing the Influence cost or otherwise accelerating development. Direct free Agent placement was discussed but is not preferred as the default because a permanent Agent is a substantial reward.

Working examples:
- Military: **Military Academy**;
- Arcane: **Theoretical Breakthrough / Conclave Development**;
- Religious: **Ecclesiastical Reform**;
- Merchant: **Commercial Charter**.

### Institutional Purge
A card that attacks rival Agents inside the Institution without automatically deleting them. Rival Families must **repay an Influence cost** to maintain affected Agents; Agents whose maintenance cost is not paid are expelled.

Working names:
- Military: **Officer Purge / Loyalty Commission**;
- Arcane: **Conclave Review / Purge of Charlatans**;
- Religious: **Hunt the Heretics**;
- Merchant: **Audit of Privileges / Licence Revocation**.

Exact cost per Agent, whether Agent seniority modifies that cost, and how many Agents may be affected remain provisional.

### Political Mobilization / Inclination Vote
Each Institution may contain a card that helps push Morneval toward its associated City Inclination:
- Military → Military;
- Merchant → Mercantile;
- Religious → Religious;
- Arcane → Arcane.

Preferred direction: strengthen, initiate or influence the existing Vote mechanism rather than directly moving the Inclination track without a Vote. Exact implementation remains unresolved.

### Temporary Bid Influence
Arcane and Religious Intrigue were proposed as possible sources of **temporary Influence usable only for a specific Stake auction**. This temporary Influence may support the bid and, if the player wins, may be used to pay the winning bid. It disappears after the auction and does not count for ordinary actions, Influence retention or First Player determination.

Possible differentiation:
- Arcane: information advantage, potentially playable reactively after bidding has begun;
- Religious: public authority, support of the faithful or threat of excommunication, potentially declared earlier.

Merchant should normally manipulate credit and auction structure instead of receiving the same straightforward temporary-Influence effect.

# A.2 — Military Intrigue

Military Intrigue should focus on coercion, seizure, security, Order and the authoritarian allocation of city resources.

## A.2.1 — Assassination
**Status:** Core concept already associated with Military / institutional political violence.

Attempt to eliminate a target such as a Family member or Agent. Family-member assassination should remain harder and uncertain. Exact target hierarchy, cost and probability remain unresolved.

## A.2.2 — Bodyguards / Protection Detail
**Status:** Proposed.

A targeted defensive reaction against **Assassination** or closely defined violent actions. It should not be a universal counter against every type of Intrigue.

This supports the broader design principle that defensive cards protect against specific threats rather than functioning as generic “cancel anything” cards.

## A.2.3 — Repossession / Military Requisition of Hinterland Land
**Status:** Proposed.

A coercive takeover or replacement effect aimed specifically at a **Hinterland / Raw-Resource Stake**. This is the Military version of the broader “take control” mechanical family.

Exact replacement cost, compensation, eligible targets and interaction with permanent Raw-Resource Stakes remain unresolved.

## A.2.4 — Martial Law
**Status:** Proposed.

Temporarily improve **Order** or mitigate an Unrest-related effect. This printed effect should preferably focus on Order rather than duplicate the generic Military Intrigue contribution, which already provides temporary Force.

## A.2.5 — Military Academy
**Status:** Proposed shared development-family card.

Reduce the cost of developing the Military Institution or otherwise accelerate Military institutional development. Exact discount remains provisional.

## A.2.6 — Officer Purge / Loyalty Commission
**Status:** Proposed shared purge-family card.

Rival Families with Agents in the Military Institution must repay an Influence cost to keep affected Agents. Unpaid Agents are expelled.

## A.2.7 — Military Political Mobilization
**Status:** Proposed shared vote-family card.

Facilitate or strengthen a Vote pushing Morneval toward **Military Inclination**. Exact Vote bonus remains to be designed.

## A.2.8 — Food Requisition
**Status:** Strongly developed concept; numerical details still require testing.

Before normal demand resolution, the Military authority **requisitions all available Food production**.

Proposed resolution:
1. Requisitioned Food is allocated first to **Population Food Demand**.
2. Any excess Food is placed in a **Granary** and is available during the following Generation.
3. Food remaining in the Granary after that following Generation is lost; the Granary does not create indefinite multi-generation stockpiling.
4. The **Population-feeding Prestige normally associated with the requisitioned Food is awarded to the Family that played Food Requisition**, representing political credit for feeding the city, rather than to the normal Production-Stake owners.
5. Requisitioned Food cannot also be sold to Institutions or External Markets during the same Generation.
6. Stored Food is municipal reserve when used later: it should not generate Wealth or duplicate production/land Prestige a second time.

This card can protect Morneval from famine while depriving producers of more lucrative customers, creating a deliberate conflict between city security and private economic interest.

# A.3 — Arcane Intrigue

Arcane Intrigue should emphasize intelligence, information, counters, exceptional manipulation, technology and temporary productive enhancement. Arcane is envisioned as the Institution with the strongest concentration of **counterplay**, but counters should normally have defined targets rather than cancel every possible card.

## A.3.1 — Intelligence / Spy Network
**Status:** Proposed identity card.

A spy network discovers another Family's Intrigue before or as it is used. Several implementations were discussed:
- cancel the action of another Intrigue card;
- reveal or inspect an opponent's card/hand;
- force an opponent to pay additional Influence to maintain the played effect;
- reduce rather than completely cancel an effect.

The simplest working concept is a card that can **cancel a defined category of Intrigue** because the Arcane network discovered the plan in advance.

## A.3.2 — Counter-Intelligence
**Status:** Proposed effect family.

Arcane may have several **specialized counters** rather than one universal cancellation card. Candidate targets mentioned include:
- Assassination;
- takeover/requisition;
- Vote manipulation;
- Institutional Purge.

Exact partition between Intelligence and Counter-Intelligence cards remains unresolved.

## A.3.3 — Theoretical Breakthrough / Technological Acceleration
**Status:** Proposed.

Reduce the cost of a technological development or Production-Sector advancement for the Generation. This should connect to the future technology / Production-tier development system once that system is finalized.

## A.3.4 — Hinterland Enhancement
**Status:** Proposed.

Temporarily increase the production capacity of one Hinterland tile or raw-resource source for the Generation.

This may eventually become a specific printed card even if Arcane's generic contribution uses technological cost reduction, or vice versa.

## A.3.5 — Conclave Development
**Status:** Proposed shared development-family card.

Reduce the cost of developing the Arcane Institution or otherwise accelerate its institutional development.

## A.3.6 — Conclave Purge / Review of the Conclave
**Status:** Proposed shared purge-family card.

Rival Families must repay Influence to retain affected Agents inside the Arcane Institution; Agents not maintained are expelled.

## A.3.7 — Arcane Political Manipulation
**Status:** Proposed shared vote-family card.

Facilitate or strengthen a Vote pushing Morneval toward **Arcane Inclination**.

## A.3.8 — Insider Information / Bid Advantage
**Status:** Proposed overlap with Religious Intrigue.

Gain temporary Influence usable only for a Stake auction. The Arcane version should represent superior information and may be distinguished by **late/reactive timing**, e.g. being played after one or more bids have already been made.

## A.3.9 — Knowledge / Event Manipulation
**Status:** Early idea only.

Arcane cards may eventually generate Knowledge, alter an Event/result, or permit unusual rule manipulation. No specific final card text has yet been defined.

# A.4 — Religious Intrigue

Religious Intrigue should emphasize legitimacy, moral authority, social discipline, reputation, charity and pressure through the faithful.

## A.4.1 — Hunt the Heretics
**Status:** Proposed shared purge-family card.

A religious purge inside the Religious Institution. Rival Families must repay Influence to maintain affected Agents; those that are not maintained are expelled.

## A.4.2 — Ecclesiastical Reform
**Status:** Proposed shared development-family card.

Reduce the cost of developing the Religious Institution or otherwise accelerate its institutional development.

## A.4.3 — Religious Mobilization / Sermon
**Status:** Proposed shared vote-family card.

Facilitate or strengthen a Vote pushing Morneval toward **Religious Inclination**.

## A.4.4 — Support of the Faithful / Threat of Excommunication
**Status:** Proposed overlap with Arcane bid support.

Gain temporary Influence usable only for a Stake auction. The Religious version represents public authority, organized support or the threat of excommunication. Unlike Arcane's information-based version, this support may be more visible and declared earlier.

## A.4.5 — Ecclesiastical Confiscation
**Status:** Early overlap concept.

A Religious version of a seizure/takeover effect was proposed as an example of controlled mechanical overlap: confiscation, forced donation or religious judgment could remove or transfer an asset. The final target should differ from the Military land-requisition identity if retained.

## A.4.6 — Reputation / Legitimacy Protection
**Status:** Early effect family.

Religious Intrigue may protect Prestige/reputation, delegitimize another Family, or defend against a limited class of political Intrigue. No exact card text is yet defined.

# A.5 — Merchant Intrigue

Merchant Intrigue should emphasize commercial manipulation, credit, auctions, demand, privileged contracts and the redirection of production toward private profit.

## A.5.1 — Hostile Takeover of a Production Stake
**Status:** Proposed effect family; direct version may be superseded by more interesting auction manipulation.

Take over or facilitate replacement of a rival **Production Stake**, possibly through a reduced replacement premium. This is the Merchant counterpart to Military seizure of Hinterland land.

The design discussion increasingly favored manipulating the acquisition/auction process rather than simply stealing an existing Stake, but the takeover concept remains recorded.

## A.5.2 — Binding Bids / All Bidders Pay
**Status:** Strongly developed concept.

Before a specified Stake auction begins, change the auction's payment rule:

> **Every bidder pays their final committed bid, even if they lose the Stake.**

Important proposed clarifications:
- the card must be played **before the first bid**, not after seeing large bids;
- a player pays only their **final committed bid**, not the sum of every intermediate raise;
- the rule applies to the player who played the card as well as to rivals;
- temporary bid-only Influence may pay obligations from that same auction if applicable.

The purpose is to transform a normally low-risk auction into a genuine commitment, making players hesitate before bidding aggressively.

## A.5.3 — Credit Line / Overcommitment
**Status:** Strongly developed concept.

The Merchant Family may bid more total Influence across simultaneous/pending auctions than it actually possesses, allowing it to remain active in several auctions at once.

At resolution:
1. the player chooses which won auctions to **honor**;
2. honored auctions must be paid in full with actual available Influence;
3. each won auction the player chooses not to honor is a **default**;
4. each default costs **1 Prestige** (accepted working penalty);
5. the defaulted Stake passes to the next eligible / second-highest bidder at that bidder's final bid, rather than reopening the auction.

The card represents credit, leverage and conditional financial commitments. The Prestige penalty prevents risk-free fake bidding designed only to inflate opponents' prices.

## A.5.4 — Private Buyer
**Status:** Strongly developed concept.

Before normal demand resolution, choose **1 unit of production generated by one of your Production Stakes** and sell it immediately to a private buyer for **2 Wealth**.

Proposed consequences:
- that production unit is removed from the supply available for normal Population / Institution / External Market demand resolution;
- the selected Stake is considered used/served by the private sale and cannot satisfy another demand that Generation;
- the sale consumes the relevant raw-resource capacity;
- if used on Food, the diversion may deliberately create a Food shortage and contribute to famine/Population loss;
- productive Hinterland whose capacity was actually consumed still counts as used for the normal productive-land Prestige rule.

The card's power is not a higher-than-market return, but the ability to **prioritize a private sale before the city's normal allocation rules**.

## A.5.5 — Commercial Charter
**Status:** Proposed shared development-family card.

Reduce the cost of developing the Merchant Institution or otherwise accelerate its institutional development.

## A.5.6 — Audit of Privileges / Licence Revocation
**Status:** Proposed shared purge-family card.

Rival Families must repay Influence to maintain affected Agents inside the Merchant Institution; unpaid Agents are expelled.

## A.5.7 — Merchant Lobbying
**Status:** Proposed shared vote-family card.

Facilitate or strengthen a Vote pushing Morneval toward **Mercantile Inclination**.

## A.5.8 — Preferential Contracts / Demand Manipulation
**Status:** Early effect family.

Merchant Intrigue may manipulate demand allocation, create preferential contracts or otherwise redirect who receives production. Exact card text remains unresolved.

# A.6 — Cross-Institution overlap map

The intended overlap is controlled rather than symmetrical. Similar mechanical ideas should generally attack or support different layers of Morneval.

| Mechanical family | Military | Arcane | Religious | Merchant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Institutional development | Military Academy | Conclave Development | Ecclesiastical Reform | Commercial Charter |
| Institutional purge | Officer Purge | Conclave Purge | Hunt the Heretics | Licence Revocation |
| Favor associated Inclination | Military Mobilization | Arcane Manipulation | Religious Mobilization | Merchant Lobbying |
| Direct counter / defence | Bodyguards vs assassination | Intelligence / specialized counters | Reputation / limited protection | Not a core identity |
| Seize / displace control | Hinterland land | Primarily counter/manipulate | Possible confiscation | Production Stake |
| Auction advantage | Not a core identity | Temporary bid Influence / information | Temporary bid Influence / authority | Auction-rule manipulation, credit and overcommitment |
| Production allocation | Food Requisition | Hinterland enhancement | Generic Food-demand relief | Private Buyer / demand manipulation |

This table is a design map, not a requirement that every cell receive a card.

# A.7 — Generic Intrigue uses already in the core rules

These are not individual printed cards, but every card from the corresponding Institution may be discarded for the Institution's generic temporary city contribution under MOR-4.17:

- **Merchant:** temporarily increase Renown for External Market demand; exact diminishing-return thresholds unresolved.
- **Military:** contribute to temporary City Force; locked thresholds are **1–3 cards = +1 Force, 4–6 = +2, 7–9 = +3**, continuing in the same pattern if necessary.
- **Religious:** temporarily reduce Population Food Demand; exact diminishing-return thresholds and maximum reduction unresolved.
- **Arcane:** temporary technology/productivity support; final standard form and thresholds unresolved, with technological-development discount and Hinterland-capacity increase both under consideration.

# A.8 — Design principles retained for later card development

1. Start with approximately **5–6 distinct effects per Institution** rather than a large number of unique cards.
2. Duplicate physical cards may exist even if the deck contains only 5–6 distinct card texts.
3. Aim roughly for **4 strongly identifying effects + 1–2 overlapping effect families** per Institution.
4. Overlap is desirable when the same mechanical family is applied to **different targets, timings or thematic means**.
5. Defensive cards should normally be **targeted**, not universal negations.
6. Intrigue should primarily **bend existing Morneval systems** — Stakes, Agents, Votes, demand, auctions, development, Order, Force — rather than create separate mini-games.
7. Intrigue cards should remain situational enough that their generic city contribution is meaningful, but their printed effects should usually be more attractive when the right opportunity arises.
8. Exact costs, copies per deck, timing windows and numerical strengths remain playtest parameters unless separately locked.
