# Appendix B Amendment — Demand Rewards v0.11.16 Test

Status: **active balance-test hypothesis; not yet promoted over the consolidated Appendix B reward table.**

This amendment tests a destination-specific reward for each Production Stake that successfully serves one unit of demand during Economy Resolution.

| Demand served | Prestige to Stake owner | Wealth capacity generated |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Population** | **+1** | **0** |
| **Imperial** | **0** | **0** |
| **External Market** | **+1** | **+1** |

Clarifications:

- The reward belongs to the Family owning the Stake that actually serves the demand unit.
- Population demand therefore represents civic reputation rather than private income.
- External Market demand is the premium commercial outlet: it provides both reputation and income capacity.
- Imperial demand remains an obligation and gives no direct Family reward; failing Imperial demand can still create the normal collective penalty.
- Family base Wealth capacity remains unchanged.

## Automated-player interpretation

For the balance test, automated Families evaluate a prospective External Market service as carrying both its Prestige and Wealth value. This is an AI valuation rule only; it does not alter the tabletop reward.

The purpose of the test is to determine whether the Mercantile economic path becomes a credible alternative to the Military/Population path without making external trade an automatic dominant strategy.
